Privacy Policy

How Hero Drone handles your data on the website, on WhatsApp and in customer service.

Who is responsible for the data

Hero Drone, registered in Brazil under CNPJ 52.075.318/0001-35 and operating in Curitiba and its metropolitan region, is the controller of the data processed in the channels described here.

To talk about privacy or exercise your rights, write to contato@herodrone.com.br or message us on WhatsApp at +55 41 98453-7827.

What data we process

  • Forms and conversations: name, WhatsApp, email, city, type of space, service of interest, goal of the material and the content of the messages.
  • Companies we may contact: company name, job title, and business email and phone publicly available online, with the source recorded.
  • Website visits: pages visited, traffic source and technical browser data, collected by the Metricool analytics tool.
  • Contracted services: proposal, scheduling, payment and invoice data.
  • Drone shoots: images of the contracted location, which may incidentally include people or license plates.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Replying and preparing proposals: steps prior to a contract, at your request.
  • Providing the service and billing: performance of a contract and legal and tax obligations.
  • Introducing Hero Drone to companies: legitimate interest, using public business data, with the option to opt out at any time.
  • Talking on WhatsApp after you fill in a form: your consent.
  • Improving the website and communication: legitimate interest, using usage metrics.

Customer service with artificial intelligence

Part of the service on WhatsApp and email may be handled by Ava, an artificial intelligence assistant acting on behalf of Emerson, from Hero Drone. She answers questions, organizes information and records the contact in our management system.

The assistant runs on Hero Drone's own computer. Decisions such as quotes and hiring are always made by a person, and you can ask for a human at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We only share what is needed with providers that make our operation possible:

  • WhatsApp (Meta), for conversations;
  • Google, for email and authentication;
  • Cloudflare, for the customer management system;
  • Vercel, for website hosting;
  • Metricool, for website and social media metrics;
  • Accounting, payment providers and authorities, when required.

Some of these providers may process data outside Brazil, following the international transfer rules of Brazil's data protection law (LGPD).

How long we keep it

  • Assistant technical logs: up to 14 days.
  • Outreach contacts with no reply: up to 12 months.
  • Leads who did not hire us: up to 24 months after the last contact.
  • Clients: for the period required by legal, tax and contractual obligations.
  • Opt-out requests: we keep only what is needed to make sure you are not contacted again.

Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018), you can ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion of data, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To stop receiving messages, just reply asking to opt out. If needed, you can also contact Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Security

We use access control, secure connections and a private network for internal systems. No system is completely immune to incidents. If a relevant incident occurs, we will take the measures and send the notices required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this text when our services change. The version published here is always the latest.
